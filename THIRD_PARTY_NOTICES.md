# Third-party and research acknowledgements

TokenWise is an academic integration project informed by and/or adapted from the following research/software assets:

- **SWE-Pruner: Self-Adaptive Context Pruning for Coding Agents** — Yuhang Wang et al. The included neural-pruner runtime/model configuration follows the SWE-Pruner architecture and its Qwen3-Reranker-0.6B backbone approach. See `docs/papers/SWE-Pruner.pdf` and the model card files under `swe-pruner/swe-pruner/model/`.
- **SEALing the Gap: A Reference Framework for LLM Inference Carbon Estimation via Multi-Benchmark Driven Embodiment** — Priyavanshi Pathania et al. TokenWise's carbon layer is SEAL-derived and uses its own local benchmark-processing/training artifacts. See `docs/papers/SEAL-Carbon-Estimation.pdf`.
- **Qwen3-Reranker-0.6B / Qwen tokenizer assets** — model/tokenizer files are distributed subject to the terms of their original provider. The large model weight is not included in the TokenWise ZIP.
